# Problem Statement

Modern educational institutions often collect large volumes of academic data—including attendance logs, continuous internal assessments, and end-semester examinations—across disconnected spreadsheets and legacy record books.

This fragmentation leads to:
1. Late identification of academically struggling or disengaged students.
2. Lack of transparent, explainable early warning indicators.
3. High administrative burden on faculty calculating manual cohort statistics.
4. Absence of personalized, actionable study recommendations for students.
