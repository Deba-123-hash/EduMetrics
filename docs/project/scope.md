# Project Scope

## In-Scope Capabilities:
- Role-based portals for Admin, Faculty, and Students.
- 22 normalized PostgreSQL tables tracking departments, classes, subjects, exams, marks, and attendance.
- Rule-based composite risk assessment and AI-synthesized narrative insights.
- CSV exports and audit compliance logging.

## Out-of-Scope (Boundaries):
- Direct automated punitive actions or administrative disciplinary dismissals.
- Proprietary university ERP payment gateway integrations.
